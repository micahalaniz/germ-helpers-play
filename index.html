Two games

🫙 Germ Sort

Put each silly germ in the matching jar. Four levels build skills step by step: Match 2, Match 3, Colors and Count 1–5.

🧼 Hand Washing

Learn the real routine: wet, soap, scrub for 20 seconds, rinse, dry. A picture schedule stays on screen. Levels fade the support: Follow along, Pick from 2, Pick from 3, All 5 steps.

Made for students who need things calm and clear

  • First / Then card before every game (first the game, then a bubble break).
  • Predictable: stars across the top show how many turns are left. No timers to beat, no losing.
  • No "wrong" buzzer. A mistake dims that choice and the right one glows, so every turn ends in success.
  • Read aloud: every instruction is spoken and shown as words. Tap Dr. Lymph's bubble to hear it again.
  • Calm motion setting, soft sounds, sound and voice switches.
  • Works with a mouse, a touch screen, or a keyboard or switch (Tab or arrows to move, Space or Enter to choose).
  • Hand colors change between games, so every student can see hands like theirs.

For teachers: Teacher Corner

On the title screen, hold the Teacher Corner button. Add students by initials, pick each game's level, turns per game (5 or 10) and the scrub timer. During a game, the Help button gives a prompt (it highlights the answer).

Every turn is recorded like IEP trial data: I = right on their own, P = right with a prompt, E = got it after a mistake. Teacher Corner shows a progress chart and recent games for each student, and Copy all data puts everything in your clipboard to paste into a spreadsheet for progress reports.

Privacy: the data is saved only in this browser on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere: no accounts, no internet connection needed after loading, no tracking. Use initials, not names. Clearing the browser's data erases it, so copy it to a spreadsheet regularly.

Tips

  • Home during a game is a hold button, so a quick tap doesn't end the session.
  • Runs best in Chrome or Edge. The first load downloads about 10 MB.
  • Hand-washing steps follow the CDC's guidance ("When and How to Wash Your Hands").
